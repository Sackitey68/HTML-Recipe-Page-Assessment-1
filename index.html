Banku and Grilled Tilapia

A beloved Ghanaian classic featuring fermented corn-cassava dumpling served with smoky grilled tilapia and fiery pepper sauce — a true taste of West Africa!

Recipe Details

Prep Time: 30 minutes

Cook Time: 25 minutes

Servings: 4 - 6 people

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

For the Banku:

  • 2 cups fermented corn dough
  • ½ cup fermented cassava dough
  • 2-3 cups water (adjust for consistency)
  • Salt to taste

For the Grilled Tilapia:

  • 2 whole tilapia fish, cleaned and scaled
  • 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, minced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2-3 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 2 ripe tomatoes, blended
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Banku: Mix fermented corn dough and cassava dough with water in a large pot until smooth. Add salt to taste.
  2. Place pot over medium-high heat and stir constantly with a wooden spoon to prevent lumps. Reduce heat and continue stirring vigorously for 15-20 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  3. Marinate the Tilapia: Score cleaned fish on both sides. Mix ginger, garlic, half the onions, salt, and pepper. Rub marinade all over fish and inside cavity. Let marinate for 15-20 minutes.
  4. Grill the Fish: Brush fish with vegetable oil and grill over medium-high heat for 10-12 minutes per side until crispy and cooked through.
  5. Serve: Shape banku into balls using a wet spoon. Serve hot with grilled tilapia and pepper sauce on the side.

💡 Tips & Notes

  • For authentic flavor, use properly fermented dough (2-3 days fermentation). Pre-fermented dough is available in African markets.
  • Stir the banku continuously to achieve the signature smooth, stretchy texture without lumps.
  • Adjust scotch bonnet peppers according to your heat preference — they're very spicy!
  • Traditionally eaten with your hands: tear off a piece of banku, dip in pepper sauce, and enjoy with flaked fish.
